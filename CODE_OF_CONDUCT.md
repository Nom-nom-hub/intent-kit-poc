# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Intent Kit community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include communicating with empathy, respecting differing viewpoints, giving and accepting constructive feedback, taking responsibility for mistakes, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include sexualized language or imagery, trolling or insulting comments, public or private harassment, publishing another person’s private information without permission, and conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces and when an individual officially represents the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior through a private GitHub Security Advisory or by contacting a project maintainer through the repository owner’s GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with appropriate confidentiality.

Maintainers may issue a correction, warning, temporary ban, or permanent ban in response to behavior that violates these standards.
